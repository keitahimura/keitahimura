Philosophy

Breaking Boundaries

I enjoy being curious and exploring art in various forms, may it be digital or traditional. I am someone that firmly believes art is a form of expression that represents existence.

I believe in timeless aesthetics that stand out yet remain elegant. Every detail matters, from the spacing between letters to the choice of colors that define a mood.

School Timeline

Education Over the Years

2022 - 2023

High School

ADT Montessori School

I have been studying at ADT Montessori School ever since I was in nursery, so you could say that it was my 2nd home. there were good memories here and made friends. but, I wouldn't choose to time travel and choose that timeline again. it was a bit a dark moment for me for as my younger self was a naive girl back then. that path led me to change drastically over the years that I became someone who is "way too mature" for my age.

2024 - 2026

Senior High School

CASAP College of Arts and Sciences of Asia and the Pacific

During my time at CASAP, I have been exposed to a different social group that I wasn't accustomed to. it didn't made me feel fit in at first until I had to learn how to adapt to other social groups and learned to understand theim well. with the artistic things I knew, it made me join a specific club that that could help me improve my skills in terms of art while still enjoying something I love.

2026 - Present

College

CIIT College of Innovation and Integrated Technology

Currently pursuing my college education at CIIT as it has been my dream school since 7th grade and with the influence of my older sister who is a graduate at CIIT. I am immersing myself in the world of technology and innovation and also seeing the diverse academic environment made me feel overwhelmed but in a good way. its way of education was very different compared to my old schools I attended. the teaching styles here are really helpful especially for visual learners like me. I was able to learn new things about programming, design, and art that wasn't taught to me back then.
